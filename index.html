The Story

About Me

From science fiction fascination to engineering autonomous machines in the real world.

Turning science fiction into physical engineering.

Ever since I was a kid, I was completely hooked on science fiction. I devoured stories of autonomous rovers exploring uncharted planets, cybernetic systems that could perceive and adapt, and machines capable of navigating hostile worlds on their own. But just dreaming about sci-fi wasn't enough for me—I became obsessed with the idea of actually building it.

That obsession drove my entire academic path: I graduated in Robotics Engineering and pushed straight into an advanced specialization in Artificial Intelligence. Through rigorous engineering studies and master's research, I dove into the gritty fundamentals—control theory, processor architectures, sensor integration, and real-time firmware in C/C++.

Today, I bridge that hardware foundation with bleeding-edge AI: from implementing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) so simulated rovers make autonomous decisions in Gazebo, to fusing high-frequency RGB and LiDAR streams under punishing real-world weather, and deploying optimized neural models directly onto NVIDIA Jetson edge boards.

Education: Degree in Robotics Engineering • Advanced Specialization & Master's Research in Artificial Intelligence.
The Spark: Sci-fi made me fall in love with intelligent machines; engineering gave me the power to build them.
My Mission: Pulling AI out of the cloud and giving it a body that can reason, navigate, and touch reality.
